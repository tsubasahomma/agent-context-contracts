# Scratch Protocol

Use this protocol for transient notes, intermediate tool output, exploratory
observations, local drafts, generated summaries, and other material that has not
been promoted into a durable artifact.

## Scratch Rules

- Scratch is non-authoritative.
- Scratch may guide what to inspect next, but it cannot govern scope, prove
  validation, establish local policy, or replace current inspected evidence.
- Scratch must not contain secrets, unredacted sensitive material, or broad raw
  transcripts when a bounded evidence pointer would be safer.
- Do not introduce a repository-local scratch directory as a coordination or
  handoff mechanism.

## Reuse Boundary

Before scratch can support a material claim, promote the relevant observation
through `promote-memory.md` into the correct durable destination with:

- subject and scope;
- source evidence or inspected-state pointer;
- observation point and freshness;
- omitted surfaces and limitations;
- validation status when validation is claimed.

Discard scratch when it is stale, unsupported, unrelated to active scope, or too
broad to review safely.
